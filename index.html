As the Principal of Cumilla Polytechnic Institute, Engr. Muhammad Majharul Islam Chowdhury believes that true education goes beyond textbooks—it builds discipline, responsibility, and innovation in every learner. He always advises students to focus not only on grades, but also on developing technical skills, moral values, and problem-solving abilities. According to him, the future belongs to those who are honest, hardworking, and creative in their field. In today’s competitive world, he emphasizes the importance of practical learning and teamwork, reminding students that success comes through perseverance and continuous self-improvement.
